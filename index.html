About Suvana Construction LLC

Suvana Construction LLC is a Middletown-based handyman and general contracting company focused on delivering professional, dependable, and affordable services to homeowners and small businesses across New Castle County.

We specialize in fast-response repairs, small to medium remodeling projects, and custom carpentry. Our team places a strong emphasis on clear communication, quality materials, and tidy job sites. Whether you need a one-off repair or recurring maintenance, we tailor our services to fit your schedule and budget.
